patchworks are more difficult puzzles than standard crosswords! you are in for a challenge.
instead of across and down clues, there are now spanners and patches.

==> SPANNERS
spanners are the across clues, but they are all 15 letters long. each clue has two parts. A // B, which clue two entirely different things.
for example, the clue "happy // sad" could be "DELIGHTEDGLOOMY"

==> PATCHES
patches are the regions indicated by the bold lines and are always read in standard reading order, no matter how strange that may look.
for example,
+     +-----+-----+-----+
   H  |  E     X     A  |
+-----+     +-----+     +
|  M     P  |  E  |  L  |
+     +-----+     +-----+
|  E  |  L     O     W   
+-----+     +     +     +
the indicated patch would be read as EXAMPLE, despite having a gap between the P and the L.

==> DOWN CLUES?
there are none. the columns will make absolutely nothing when read like down clues.
